What's going on

Gold is $3,668 an ounce and jumped 2.9% today.
  • That is almost exactly where the plan expected it ($3,618). Nothing surprising yet.
  • US inflation is still 3.3% — well above the 2% the Fed wants. So the Fed keeps raising interest rates, and that is the single biggest thing pushing gold down.
  • Oil is $91 — middling. Not adding to inflation, not helping either.
  • The US dollar is strong (104). A strong dollar makes gold more expensive everywhere else, which caps any rally.
  • AI-linked companies are in real trouble — Oracle is down 57% from its peak. If their debts start failing, the Fed would be forced to cut rates, which would send gold sharply higher. Watch this.
  • Most likely path right now (45%): The AI bubble bursts. If that happens — gold dips hard first, then jumps a long way.
